🌯 Is Chipotle Gluten Free?

Everything you need to know about eating gluten-free at Chipotle Mexican Grill

Home > Guides > Is Chipotle Gluten Free?

📅 Last Updated: October 23, 2026

✅ Yes, Almost Everything Except the Flour Tortillas

Chipotle's own allergen chart lists gluten in exactly one thing: the flour tortillas (burrito and taco size). Every protein, both rices, both beans, the salsas, queso blanco, the chips and the crispy corn taco shells are listed without gluten. For celiac disease the risk is cross-contact on the line, which you can reduce by asking for a glove change.

Most people searching this have a specific item in mind: the queso, the chips, the corn tortillas, the brown rice. The short answers are below, all checked against Chipotle's published allergen chart in October 2026. Chipotle changes its menu, and limited-time items can carry allergens that aren't on the standard chart, so check the chart again before ordering anything new.

🧀 Quick Answers: Queso, Tortillas, Chips, Rice

Queso blanco: No gluten. Chipotle lists dairy as its only allergen.

Corn tortillas and crispy corn taco shells: No gluten ingredients. Chipotle notes that corn items may carry trace gluten picked up in the field (more on that below).

Flour tortillas: Contain wheat. This is the only gluten item on the chart.

Tortilla chips: No gluten ingredients. Same corn caveat as the tortillas.

White and brown rice: Both gluten-free.

Chipotle-honey vinaigrette: No gluten. Its only flagged allergen is sulfites, from vinegar.

🌯 Everything on the Chart Without Gluten

Proteins: chicken, steak, barbacoa, carnitas, sofritas (sofritas contain soy).

Rice and beans: white rice, brown rice, black beans, pinto beans.

Salsas and sauces: fresh tomato salsa, roasted chili-corn salsa, tomatillo green-chili salsa, tomatillo red-chili salsa, red chimichurri sauce.

Toppings: fajita vegetables, romaine, supergreens lettuce blend, Monterey Jack cheese, sour cream, guacamole, queso blanco.

Shells and sides: corn tortillas, crispy corn tortillas, tortilla chips, chipotle-honey vinaigrette.

⚠️ The One Item to Avoid

Flour tortillas, in both burrito and taco sizes. That's the only gluten-containing item on Chipotle's standard menu. A burrito bowl or salad avoids them entirely.

🌽 The Corn Caveat Chipotle Publishes

Chipotle's allergen page says that items containing corn (the corn tortillas, crispy corn tortillas, chips and corn salsa) may have trace amounts of gluten from mixing with gluten-containing grains in the field. That's a sourcing issue, not a kitchen one, and it's unusual for a chain to state it so plainly.

Most people with celiac disease eat those items without trouble. If you're among the most sensitive, a bowl with no chips, no corn salsa and no corn tortillas removes that variable.

⚠️ Cross-Contact on the Line

Chipotle says ingredients can come into contact with each other during preparation and that it can't guarantee the absence of allergens. In practice the concern is the line itself: the same staff handle flour tortillas and then build your bowl, and flour tortillas are warmed in the same area as corn ones.

Chipotle's published fix is simple: if you're highly sensitive, ask them to change gloves at the start of your order. That's their wording, and staff are used to the request.

📋 How to Order at Chipotle with Celiac Disease

  1. Say it first: "I have celiac disease. Could you change your gloves before you start?"
  2. Pick a bowl or salad. No flour tortilla in the picture at all.
  3. Choose any protein, rice and beans. None of them contain gluten.
  4. Add salsas, cheese, sour cream, guacamole or queso. All are listed without gluten.
  5. Watch the line. If you see a scoop dip from the tortilla area into a bin, it's reasonable to ask for a fresh scoop or skip that item.
  6. Very sensitive? Skip the corn items, per Chipotle's own field-contact note.

🌮 Corn Tortilla Tacos or a Bowl?

The corn tortillas themselves are fine. The extra risk is handling: they're warmed and assembled next to the flour tortillas. If tacos are what you want, ask for the glove change and watch how they're heated. If you'd rather not think about it, a bowl gets you the same fillings with fewer touch points.

🙋 Frequently Asked Questions

Is Chipotle gluten free?

Almost entirely. Chipotle's allergen chart lists gluten only in its flour tortillas. Everything else on the standard menu is listed without gluten, though cross-contact on the line is possible, so ask for a glove change if you have celiac disease.

Is Chipotle queso gluten free?

Yes. Queso blanco is listed with dairy as its only allergen.

Are Chipotle tortillas gluten free?

The corn tortillas and crispy corn taco shells are. The flour tortillas are not. Chipotle notes that corn items may carry trace gluten from the field.

Are Chipotle chips gluten free?

They have no gluten ingredients. Like the corn tortillas, they fall under Chipotle's note about possible trace gluten from mixing in the field.

Is Chipotle brown rice gluten free?

Yes. Both the white and brown rice are listed without gluten.

Are Chipotle tacos gluten free?

Yes, if you order them on corn tortillas or crispy corn shells. Taco-size flour tortillas contain wheat.

Is the chipotle-honey vinaigrette gluten free?

Yes. Its only listed allergen is sulfites, from the vinegar.

💡 Final Verdict

✅ One of the Easiest Chains for Gluten-Free Eating

With gluten in a single item, Chipotle is about as simple as fast food gets for celiacs. Order a bowl, ask for a glove change, and you have a full meal without a list of exceptions. The cautions are cross-contact on a shared line and Chipotle's own note about trace gluten in corn.
