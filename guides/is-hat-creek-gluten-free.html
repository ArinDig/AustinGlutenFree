🍔 Is Hat Creek Gluten Free?

Austin's own burger chain — and one of the best fast-casual GF options in the city.

Home > Guides > Is Hat Creek Gluten Free?

📅 Last Updated: June 2026

✅ Yes — One of Austin's Best Fast-Casual GF Options

Hat Creek uses dedicated fryers for fries, onion rings, tater tots, fried pickles, and chicken nuggets. A gluten-free bun is available on any burger. This is genuinely exceptional for a fast-casual chain.

Hat Creek Burger Company is an Austin-born chain, and it turns out it's also one of the most gluten-free-friendly fast-casual burger spots in the city. Dedicated fryers, a GF bun option, and a published gluten-free menu page set it apart from most competitors. If you've been defaulting to Chick-fil-A for a safe fast-casual meal, Hat Creek is worth knowing about.

🍟 What Makes Hat Creek Stand Out for GF Diners

✅ Dedicated Fryers — Multiple Items

Hat Creek uses dedicated fryers for their gluten-free items, meaning the frying oil is not shared with breaded, wheat-containing products. This covers french fries, tater tots, sweet potato fries, onion rings, fried pickles, and chicken nuggets — an unusually broad set of fried items that are safe for celiac diners.

Most fast-casual chains protect fries at best. Hat Creek protects everything fried on their GF menu. That's a meaningful operational commitment.

✅ Gluten-Free Bun Available

Hat Creek offers a gluten-free bun as a substitute on any burger or chicken sandwich. This means you can order a complete burger — not a bunless patty on a tray — and eat it like a normal person. Very few fast-casual burger chains in Austin offer this.

❌ What to Avoid

  • Standard bun — contains wheat; always request the GF bun substitution
  • Any breaded item not on the GF menu page — Hat Creek publishes their GF menu at hatcreekburgers.com/free-from/gluten-free; if it's not listed there, skip it

⚠️ What About Cross-Contamination?

Hat Creek is not a certified gluten-free restaurant. The kitchen handles standard wheat buns and other gluten-containing items alongside GF preparations. The dedicated fryers eliminate the most common cross-contamination vector, but shared prep surfaces, shared grill space, and shared condiment stations are part of the environment.

For most people with celiac disease, Hat Creek's dedicated fryer setup and GF bun option make it one of the safer fast-casual choices in Austin. If you're extremely sensitive, communicate your allergy clearly when ordering — Hat Creek staff are generally accustomed to GF requests given their published GF menu.

📋 How to Order GF at Hat Creek

🏆 The Best GF Order at Hat Creek

Burger on a GF bun + onion rings + milkshake. This is the order you can't get at most fast-casual chains — a real burger on an actual bun, with fried sides that are safe. It's a complete meal, not a compromise.

  1. Ask for the GF bun when ordering any burger or chicken sandwich. It's a substitution, so mention it when you order.
  2. Mention your allergy. Hat Creek has a published GF menu, so staff are familiar with the requests. Saying "gluten allergy" ensures they take extra care.
  3. Order freely from the fried sides — fries, tots, onion rings, fried pickles are all in dedicated fryers.
  4. Get a milkshake. All four flavors are GF and made on dedicated machines.

🙋 Frequently Asked Questions

Is Hat Creek gluten free?

Does Hat Creek have gluten-free buns?

Yes. GF bun is available as a substitution on any burger or chicken sandwich. Just ask when you order.

Are Hat Creek fries gluten free?

Yes — cooked in a dedicated fryer. Same for tater tots, sweet potato fries, onion rings, and fried pickles. All GF, all dedicated fryer.

Are Hat Creek chicken nuggets gluten free?

Yes — Hat Creek nuggets (regular, buffalo, and sauced) are GF and cooked in dedicated fryers. A rare find at a fast-casual chain.

Are Hat Creek milkshakes gluten free?

Yes — all four flavors (chocolate, vanilla, strawberry, peanut butter cup) are GF and made on dedicated milkshake machines.
