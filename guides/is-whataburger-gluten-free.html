🍔 Is Whataburger Gluten Free?

Everything you need to know about eating gluten-free at Whataburger

Home > Guides > Is Whataburger Gluten Free?

📅 Last Updated: October 23, 2026

⚠️ Fries: No Wheat Listed. Buns: No GF Option.

Whataburger lists soy as the only allergen in its French Fries, so the fries have no gluten ingredients. It doesn't publish whether fries share a fryer with battered onion rings and chicken strips. There are no gluten-free buns. With celiac disease, Whataburger is workable at best.

This guide is based on the allergen panels in Whataburger's online menu and its nutrition guide, checked in October 2026. Whataburger's own statement is blunt: it doesn't operate allergen-free kitchens, and shared equipment means any item can come into contact with major allergens.

🍟 Are Whataburger Fries Gluten Free?

Ingredients: Whataburger's online menu lists one allergen for the French Fries: soy. No wheat.

The fryer: Whataburger also fries battered onion rings and breaded chicken strips, and it doesn't say whether the fries get their own fryer. That decides whether the fries are celiac-safe, and it can differ by location.

What to do: ask, "Are the fries cooked in the same oil as the onion rings or chicken strips?" If the answer is yes, or nobody knows, skip them.

Earlier versions of this guide said flatly that the fries are cooked in a shared fryer. We couldn't confirm that from Whataburger, so we've changed it to what Whataburger actually publishes.

🍔 Does Whataburger Have Gluten-Free Buns?

No. Whataburger's menu and nutrition guide list no gluten-free bun. A burger without the bun is possible, but buns are toasted and handled on the same line, so ask for fresh gloves and expect some cross-contact risk.

❌ What to Skip

  • All buns, Texas Toast and biscuits
  • Onion rings and chicken strips (battered or breaded)
  • Breakfast taquitos and burritos on flour tortillas
  • Anything you can't check: the online menu shows allergens per item under "Show Nutrition," so look before ordering

📋 If You Do Order at Whataburger

  1. Tell them it's for celiac disease and ask for fresh gloves.
  2. Order a patty with no bun, with simple toppings (lettuce, tomato, pickles, onion).
  3. Ask about the fryer before adding fries.
  4. Check any sauce in the online menu first; allergens are listed per item.

🍔 Better Gluten-Free Burgers in Austin

If you have a choice, an Austin burger spot with a real gluten-free bun is a better meal. Hopdoddy offers gluten-free buns; see our page for how to order there. For another fast-food comparison, our P. Terry's guide covers the same bun and fryer questions.

🙋 Frequently Asked Questions

Are Whataburger fries gluten free?

By ingredients, yes. Whataburger lists soy as the only allergen in its French Fries. What it doesn't publish is whether fries share a fryer with battered items like onion rings and chicken strips, so if you have celiac disease, ask at your location before ordering.

Does Whataburger have gluten-free buns?

No. Whataburger's menu and nutrition guide list no gluten-free bun. You can order a burger without a bun, but it's cooked and assembled in a kitchen where buns are toasted and handled.

Does Whataburger have gluten-free options?

A few. The fries list no wheat, and a burger patty without the bun has no bun-related gluten. Battered items (onion rings, chicken strips), buns, Texas Toast, biscuits and taquito tortillas are not options.

Is Whataburger safe for celiac disease?

It's a hard place to eat with celiac disease. Whataburger says it doesn't operate allergen-free kitchens and that shared equipment means cross-contact is possible. If you go, keep the order simple and ask about the fryer.
