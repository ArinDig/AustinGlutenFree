🍪 Is Bird Bird Biscuit Gluten Free?

GF biscuit available at all 3 Austin locations — but most sandwiches include breaded chicken. Here's the full honest breakdown.

Home > Guides > Is Bird Bird Biscuit Gluten Free?

📅 Last Updated: June 2026

⚠️ GF Biscuit Yes — But Read This First

Bird Bird Biscuit offers a gluten-free biscuit at all three Austin locations. But most of their signature sandwiches feature breaded fried chicken — which contains gluten regardless of the biscuit. Not a dedicated GF kitchen. Great for gluten sensitivity; risky for strict celiac.

Bird Bird Biscuit is one of Austin's most beloved breakfast spots — and for good reason. The biscuits are exceptional, the sandwiches are creative, and they've built a genuine cult following. If you're gluten free, the news is mostly good: a GF biscuit option exists. But the situation is more nuanced than a simple yes, and it matters which sandwich you order. This guide gives you the full picture.

🍪 The GF Biscuit: What It Is and Isn't

✅ GF Biscuit Available at All 3 Locations

Bird Bird offers a gluten-free biscuit substitute at Manor Road, Koenig Lane, and South Lamar. Select it when ordering online or at the kiosk — there's typically a small upcharge. The GF biscuit is genuinely good: crispy outside, fluffy inside, and holds up to the fillings. It's not an afterthought.

❌ Shared Kitchen — Flour Is Everywhere

Bird Bird is not a dedicated gluten-free facility. They produce large volumes of traditional wheat-flour biscuits all day at every location. Cross-contamination from airborne flour, shared equipment, and shared surfaces is a real risk. For strict celiac diners, this matters regardless of whether you order a GF biscuit.

🍗 The Filling Problem: Most Sandwiches Include Breaded Chicken

This is the detail that surprises most GF diners at Bird Bird: the GF biscuit solves the biscuit problem, but the fillings in most of their popular sandwiches contain gluten independently.

Bird Bird's signature sandwiches — the O.G., the Nana, and the Tammy — all feature breaded fried chicken. That chicken is coated in wheat flour batter. Swapping to a GF biscuit doesn't change that. If you have celiac or significant gluten sensitivity, you're still eating a gluten-containing protein even on a GF biscuit.

⚠️ The Sausage Gravy (Tammy) Is Also a Risk

The Tammy sandwich includes sausage gravy, which is traditionally thickened with flour. Confirm with staff whether the gravy is GF before ordering. Don't assume.

🎯 Who Bird Bird Works For (and Who It Doesn't)

✅ Works Well For:

  • Gluten sensitivity (not celiac)
  • Those who can tolerate cross-contact
  • Diners ordering the Kim or Julie
  • Anyone who misses real biscuit sandwiches

❌ Not Recommended For:

  • Strict celiac disease
  • Anyone who reacts to shared kitchen environments
  • Those wanting the O.G., Nana, or Tammy safely
  • Zero cross-contamination tolerance

🏆 Best GF Order at Bird Bird Biscuit

The Move: Kim Sandwich on a GF Biscuit

The Kim — egg, cheddar, avocado, and pickled jalapeños — on a GF biscuit is your cleanest option. No breaded protein, no gravy, just real ingredients on a genuinely good GF biscuit. Order online, specify the GF biscuit, mention your allergy at pickup. It's a satisfying breakfast that doesn't require you to compromise on anything important.

If you want a protein option, ask whether the Julie's sausage is GF — if it is, that's your other safe play.

📍 How to Order at Bird Bird Biscuit

Bird Bird runs a mostly self-service ordering model — no phone orders accepted:

  1. Order online at birdbirdbiscuit.com — preferred method; select GF biscuit when customizing your sandwich.
  2. Or use the in-store kiosk — same options, same GF biscuit selection available.
  3. Mention your allergy at pickup. Let them know you have a gluten allergy so staff can take extra care with your order.
  4. Order ahead. Bird Bird makes food fresh to order — ordering online 15-30 minutes before you arrive means your biscuit is warm when you pick it up.

🤔 Final Verdict: Is Bird Bird Biscuit Worth It?

For gluten-sensitive diners who can tolerate a shared kitchen environment: yes, absolutely. The GF biscuit is real, it's good, and getting a Kim or Julie sandwich on one is a genuinely satisfying Austin breakfast experience. Bird Bird has put thought into their GF accommodation and it shows.

For strict celiac diners: the honest answer is no. Between the shared kitchen, airborne flour, and the fact that most of their popular sandwiches include breaded fried chicken regardless of the biscuit, the risk is real. Zucchini Kill Bakery or Thai Fresh are better choices if you cannot tolerate any cross-contamination.

⚠️ Bottom Line

Gluten sensitivity: ✅ Go for it — GF biscuit on a Kim or Julie, order online, mention your allergy. You'll have a great breakfast.

Strict celiac: ❌ The shared kitchen and breaded chicken fillings make this too risky. Consider dedicated GF options like Zucchini Kill Bakery instead.

🙋 Frequently Asked Questions

Is Bird Bird Biscuit gluten free?

Partially. A GF biscuit is available at all three Austin locations, but most signature sandwiches include breaded fried chicken (which contains gluten). The kitchen is shared with wheat flour. Good for gluten sensitivity; not recommended for strict celiac.

Does Bird Bird Biscuit have a gluten-free biscuit?

Yes — available at Manor Road, Koenig Lane, and South Lamar for a small upcharge. Select it when ordering online or at the kiosk.

Which sandwiches are most gluten-free friendly at Bird Bird?

The Kim (egg, cheddar, avocado, pickled jalapeños) is the best option — no breaded protein. The Julie (sausage, egg, cheese) may work depending on sausage ingredients — confirm with staff. Avoid the O.G., Nana, and Tammy if you're strictly avoiding gluten.

Is the fried chicken at Bird Bird Biscuit gluten free?

No — it's breaded with wheat flour. The O.G., Nana, and Tammy all include this chicken. A GF biscuit swap does not make those sandwiches gluten free.

Is Bird Bird Biscuit safe for celiac disease?

Not recommended. Shared kitchen, airborne wheat flour from biscuit production, and breaded chicken in most sandwiches create significant cross-contamination risk. For strict celiac, look at dedicated GF options like Zucchini Kill Bakery.
