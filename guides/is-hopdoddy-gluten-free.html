🍔 Is Hopdoddy Gluten Free?

GF buns on any craft burger — one of Austin's best options for celiac diners. Here's exactly what to order (and what to skip).

Home > Guides > Is Hopdoddy Gluten Free?

📅 Last Updated: June 12, 2026

✅ Yes — Hopdoddy Has Gluten-Free Buns

Hopdoddy offers a GF bun substitution on any burger — fresh-ground beef patties are naturally GF, and lettuce wraps are always an option. The catch: fries cook in a shared fryer. Skip the fries for celiac safety.

🏆 Best GF Order at Hopdoddy

Magic Shroom or Classic Burger on a GF bun — both are crowd favorites and the toppings are naturally GF. Add a vanilla or chocolate shake (no cookie or brownie mix-ins). Skip the fries. It's a legitimately great meal, not a compromise version.

Hopdoddy is one of Austin's most-loved burger restaurants, and for gluten-free diners it stands out for a specific reason: you can actually get a real burger on a real bun. Most fast-food chains either have no GF bun at all or a sad afterthought version — Hopdoddy offers GF buns as a standard menu option, and their fresh-ground beef patties are naturally gluten-free with no fillers or binders.

The kitchen isn't dedicated GF — it's a full-scale burger operation with shared grills and shared fryers — so this isn't the right choice if you need a fully controlled environment. But for most people with celiac disease or gluten sensitivity who want a great Austin burger, Hopdoddy is the answer.

🍞 The GF Bun: What Makes Hopdoddy Different

The core of Hopdoddy's GF-friendliness is the bun substitution. This might sound like a small thing until you've spent time at fast-food chains eating bunless burgers off a tray. At Hopdoddy, you order the burger you actually want — the Magic Shroom, the Greek, the Llano Poblano — and swap to a GF bun. You get the full experience.

The GF bun is typically offered for a small upcharge (around $1–2 depending on location). It's a proper bun, not a rice cake. The texture holds up to the burger.

If you're highly sensitive or want an extra layer of safety, the lettuce wrap option eliminates bun contamination risk entirely. Hopdoddy's lettuce wraps are well-executed — crisp, substantial leaves that actually hold the burger together.

⚠️ Cross-Contamination: The Honest Picture

Shared Fryers (Main Risk)

This is the biggest issue for celiac diners at Hopdoddy: the Kennebec fries cook in shared fryers alongside breaded onion strings and other items. The fries themselves don't contain gluten, but the oil does. Do not order fries if you have celiac disease. This isn't a borderline situation — it's a clear shared-fryer cross-contamination risk.

Shared Grill Surfaces

All beef patties cook on shared grill surfaces. The grill handles beef only (no breaded items go on the grill), which makes this lower risk than the fryer situation. Most people with celiac tolerate grilled-only surfaces without issue, but it's not a dedicated GF grill.

Kitchen Environment

Hopdoddy operates full-scale burger kitchens. Wheat flour is present in buns, onion strings, and other items being prepped in the same space. Not a GF-certified facility. Most people with celiac eat here without problems using GF buns and skipping fries — but extreme sensitivity calls for the extra caution of a lettuce wrap and a direct conversation with your server.

⚠️ Fries Are Not Optional to Skip — They're a Hard No

Unlike some restaurants where "fries might be okay," Hopdoddy's shared fryer situation is clear-cut. The breaded onion strings and fries share the same oil. If you have celiac disease, skip the fries — no exceptions. This isn't about being overly cautious; it's the actual setup.

📋 How to Order GF at Hopdoddy

  1. Tell your server you have celiac disease (or a gluten allergy) when you sit down. This prompts extra care throughout prep.
  2. Request a GF bun substitution — say it explicitly when you order, not as an afterthought. Small upcharge applies.
  3. Or request a lettuce wrap if you want zero bun contamination risk. Works well with almost every burger.
  4. Ask about any burger-specific toppings — most sauces are GF but worth confirming, especially new or seasonal options.
  5. Say no to onion strings on any burger that includes them (Goodnight, some others). They're breaded — easy to remove.
  6. Skip the fries entirely. Shared fryer. Not safe. Substitute with a shake or salad.

🏙️ Austin Hopdoddy Locations

Hopdoddy has multiple Austin locations — GF buns are available at all of them, but practices can vary slightly by location and staff. The South Congress original (1400 S Congress Ave) tends to have the longest waits but is the full experience. Midday on a weekday is the easiest time to go if you want to have a conversation about your allergy without the rush.

Their fast-casual concept Lil'Doddy also offers GF buns if you want a faster, drive-through-style option.

🤔 Is Hopdoddy Worth It for Celiac Diners?

Yes — if your concern is having a real burger experience, Hopdoddy is one of the best options in Austin. The GF bun is genuine, the beef is fresh and naturally GF, and the kitchen staff at most locations handle allergen requests thoughtfully when you communicate clearly.

The fries are a hard pass. That's the trade-off. But skip the fries, get the burger you actually want on a GF bun, and add a shake without cookie pieces — and you'll have a legitimately good meal, not a compromised version of the real thing.

🙋 Frequently Asked Questions

Is Hopdoddy gluten free?

Several items are genuinely GF — any burger with a GF bun substitution, all fresh beef patties, lettuce wraps, most shakes, and standard toppings. The fries are NOT safe due to shared fryers. Not a certified GF facility, but one of the better burger options for celiac diners in Austin.

Does Hopdoddy have gluten-free buns?

Yes. GF bun substitution available on any burger for a small upcharge. This is Hopdoddy's biggest advantage over most burger chains for GF diners.

Are Hopdoddy fries gluten free?

No. Shared fryer with breaded items — cross-contamination makes them unsafe for celiac disease. Skip the fries.

What is the best GF order at Hopdoddy?

Magic Shroom or Classic Burger on a GF bun (or lettuce wrap for extra safety) + a vanilla or chocolate shake (no cookie/brownie mix-ins). Skip fries.

Is Hopdoddy safe for severe celiac?

Better than most, but not risk-free. GF buns reduce bun cross-contamination. Shared grill and kitchen environment means it's not a controlled GF facility. For severe sensitivity, a lettuce wrap + direct conversation with the manager is the safest path.

Does Hopdoddy have a lettuce wrap option?

Yes. Any burger can be wrapped in lettuce instead of a bun. This is the safest option for celiac diners who want to eliminate any bun contamination risk.

How many Hopdoddy locations are in Austin?

Multiple — including the original South Congress (1400 S Congress Ave), The Domain, and others. GF buns at all locations; practices may vary slightly, so always communicate your allergy when ordering.
