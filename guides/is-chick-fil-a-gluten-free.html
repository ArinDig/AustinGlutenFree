🍗 Is Chick-fil-A Gluten Free?

The waffle fries are safe. Here's the full breakdown of what you can and can't eat.

Home > Guides > Is Chick-fil-A Gluten Free?

📅 Last Updated: October 23, 2026

✅ Mostly: Fries, Grilled Nuggets and a GF Bun

Chick-fil-A lists no allergens in its Waffle Potato Fries, which are cooked in canola oil, while the breaded chicken is pressure-cooked separately in peanut oil. Grilled Nuggets list no allergens, and there's a Gluten Free Bun. The Mac & Cheese contains wheat.

These answers come from the ingredient and allergen panels on Chick-fil-A's own menu pages, checked in October 2026. Chick-fil-A notes that ingredients and allergens can vary by restaurant and may not reflect customizations, so treat this as a starting point and confirm with your location.

🍟 Are Chick-fil-A Fries Gluten Free?

Ingredients: potatoes, canola oil, a light coating of modified food starch and rice flour, salt and leavening. Chick-fil-A lists no allergens for them.

The oil: the waffle fries are cooked in canola oil. Chick-fil-A's breaded chicken, nuggets and strips are pressure-cooked in refined peanut oil. Different oils mean different equipment, so the fries don't sit in oil used for breaded chicken.

For celiacs: this is why Chick-fil-A's fries are one of the safer fast-food fries. Chick-fil-A doesn't label the fryer "gluten-free" or guarantee it at every restaurant, so a quick question at the counter is still smart.

An earlier version of this guide said the fries were cooked in peanut oil. That was wrong. Chick-fil-A's own page says canola.

🧀 Is Chick-fil-A Mac and Cheese Gluten Free?

No. Chick-fil-A lists milk, egg, soy and wheat in its Mac & Cheese. It's one of the most common surprises on the menu, since it looks like a simple side.

🍗 What You Can Order

  • Waffle Potato Fries: no allergens listed, canola oil.
  • Grilled Nuggets: no allergens listed. Marinated and grilled, not breaded.
  • Grilled chicken filet on the Gluten Free Bun: Chick-fil-A lists a Gluten Free Bun in its nutrition guide. The regular Grilled Chicken Sandwich comes on a multigrain brioche bun with wheat, so ask for the swap.
  • Fruit Cup and applesauce as sides.

❌ What to Skip

  • Original Chicken Sandwich, Spicy Deluxe, original nuggets and strips: breaded with wheat flour.
  • Mac & Cheese: contains wheat.
  • Regular buns, biscuits and muffins.
  • Desserts and salads: check the allergen panel for the specific item. Mix-ins, cones, croutons and some dressings contain wheat.

⚠️ Cross-Contact

Chick-fil-A is a breaded-chicken kitchen, so flour is part of the environment. The grilled chicken is cooked on a grill rather than in the pressure cookers, but the same team handles everything. If you have celiac disease, say so when you order and ask for fresh gloves. Most locations are used to the request.

📋 A Gluten-Free Order at Chick-fil-A

Grilled Nuggets + Waffle Potato Fries + a drink. Or a grilled filet on the Gluten Free Bun with a fruit cup. Say "grilled" clearly; the original nuggets come in similar packaging.

🙋 Frequently Asked Questions

Are Chick-fil-A fries gluten free?

By ingredients, yes. Chick-fil-A lists no allergens for its Waffle Potato Fries, which are potatoes cooked in canola oil with a rice-flour coating. Its breaded chicken is pressure-cooked in peanut oil, so the fries don't share oil with the breaded chicken. Fryer setups can vary, so ask your location if you have celiac disease.

Does Chick-fil-A have a dedicated gluten-free fryer?

Chick-fil-A doesn't call it a gluten-free fryer, but it cooks the waffle fries in canola oil and its breaded chicken in peanut oil, in separate equipment. That's why the fries are widely considered one of the safer fast-food fries for celiacs. Confirm at your location.

Is Chick-fil-A mac and cheese gluten free?

No. Chick-fil-A lists milk, egg, soy and wheat in its Mac & Cheese.

Does Chick-fil-A have a gluten-free bun?

Yes. Chick-fil-A lists a Gluten Free Bun in its nutrition guide. Pair it with a grilled chicken filet, not the breaded one. Availability can vary by restaurant, so ask.

Are Chick-fil-A grilled nuggets gluten free?

Yes. Chick-fil-A lists no allergens for Grilled Nuggets. The original nuggets are breaded with wheat flour, so make sure you order grilled.
