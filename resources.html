A Note on What's Not Here

You won't find gluten-digesting enzyme supplements on this list. Products like GliadinX are marketed as protection against accidental gluten exposure, but there's no clinical evidence they prevent the intestinal damage celiac disease causes, and no supplement makes cross-contact safe to risk on purpose. If you're newly diagnosed and someone recommends one of these as a safety net for eating out, talk to your gastroenterologist first.
