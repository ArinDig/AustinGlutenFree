Free Printable Gluten-Free Dining Card

Four cards per page, in English and Spanish. No email required, nothing to sign up for — print it, cut them out, and keep one in your wallet and one in the car.

The Spanish side matters more than it might seem. In a lot of Austin kitchens the person cooking your food is not the person who took your order, and a card travels back to the line in a way a spoken explanation often does not.

Getting the most out of it

  1. Print on regular paper, or on card stock if you want it to last.
  2. Cut along the dashed lines. You get four.
  3. Hand it to your server when you order and ask them to show the kitchen — that second part is what actually makes the difference.
  4. Still ask your questions out loud. The card supports the conversation; it does not replace it.

ENGLISH

I have celiac disease. Eating even a small amount of gluten makes me seriously ill.

Gluten is in wheat, barley, rye and malt — including flour tortillas, breading, soy sauce, beer, and many sauce, gravy and queso thickeners.

To prepare my meal safely, please:

  • Use clean gloves and a freshly cleaned surface
  • Use clean utensils, pans and cutting boards
  • Do not use a fryer shared with breaded foods
  • Check sauces, marinades and seasoning blends for flour

Thank you very much for your care.

ESPAÑOL

Tengo enfermedad celíaca. Comer aunque sea una pequeña cantidad de gluten me enferma gravemente.

El gluten está en el trigo, la cebada, el centeno y la malta — incluyendo tortillas de harina, empanizados, salsa de soya, cerveza y muchos espesantes de salsas y queso.

Para preparar mi comida de forma segura, por favor:

  • Use guantes limpios y una superficie recién limpiada
  • Use utensilios, sartenes y tablas de cortar limpios
  • No use una freidora compartida con alimentos empanizados
  • Revise si las salsas, adobos y sazonadores contienen harina

Muchas gracias por su atención.
