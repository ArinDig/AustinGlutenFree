⚠️ Correction (November 2026)

This page used to describe Bougie's as a 100% gluten-free, dedicated facility. That was wrong. Bougie's makes regular wheat donuts as well as gluten-free ones, and nothing on its own site describes a gluten-free kitchen. Thanks to the reader who wrote in to flag it.

About Bougie's Donuts & Coffee

Bougie's is a gourmet donut shop and full espresso bar at 5400 Brodie Lane in Sunset Valley. It makes its donuts by hand and from scratch, and it is open every day from 7:30 AM to 6 PM, or until the donuts sell out. You can order ahead for pickup or delivery through its online menu.

Gluten-Free at Bougie's

Bougie's offers gluten-free donuts, and that is the reason it is on this site. But it is a regular donut shop: wheat donuts are made in the same kitchen, and Bougie's does not publish how the gluten-free ones are kept separate.

For a bakery, that matters. Wheat flour travels through the air, and donut shops share fryers, glazes, toppings and display cases. Unless the shop tells you otherwise, assume the gluten-free donuts have some cross-contact risk.

If you have celiac disease, ask before ordering:

  • Are the gluten-free donuts made on separate equipment, or at a separate time?
  • Are they fried in their own oil, or in the same fryer as the wheat donuts?
  • Are glazes, toppings and the display case kept separate?

If you're mildly gluten-sensitive, the gluten-free donuts may be fine. If you need a fully gluten-free bakery, Austin has several: Zucchini Kill, OMG Squee, Gati Cafe and Maaribu Cafe. See our gluten-free bakery guide for the full list.
